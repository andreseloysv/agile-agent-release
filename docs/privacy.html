Privacy Policy

Agile Agent AI Plugin — Last updated: May 20, 2026

Overview

Agile Agent AI ("the Software") is designed with privacy as a core principle. The Software runs entirely on your local machine and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers operated by the Licensor.

Data Storage

All data processed by the Software is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Authentication tokens for third-party services (Jira, Confluence, GitLab, GitHub)
  • Cached API responses for performance optimization
  • Plugin configuration and preferences

Third-Party Services

The Software communicates directly with the third-party services you configure:

  • Atlassian (Jira, Confluence) — governed by Atlassian's Privacy Policy
  • GitLab — governed by GitLab's Privacy Policy
  • GitHub — governed by GitHub's Privacy Policy

The Licensor has no access to your communications with these services.

No Telemetry

The Software does not include any telemetry, analytics, or tracking mechanisms. No usage data is sent to the Licensor or any third party.

No Account Required

The Software does not require you to create an account with the Licensor. No personal information is collected during installation or use.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: support@agile-agent.ai
